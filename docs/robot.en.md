# The robot

![The snAIl prototype on the workbench](assets/images/snail.jpg){ width="520" align=right }

snAIl is a working prototype, built in our workshop. Its snail shape hides research-grade sensors and an onboard computer.

## The technology

**Omnidirectional wheel**
: A special wheel lets the robot **translate and rotate freely**, in any direction — ideal for understanding how a mobile robot moves.

**Stereo 3D camera**
: Two lenses give the robot **depth perception**, like a pair of eyes. It is the foundation of the computer-vision lessons.

**LiDAR**
: A laser scanner **maps the room** all around the robot — the basis of the path-planning lessons.

**Ultrasonics and bumpers**
: Close-range sensors and touch bumpers detect obstacles **safely**.

**Wi-Fi hotspot**
: The robot creates **its own Wi-Fi network**: the computer connects to it directly. No school network, no IT setup needed.

**Embedded AI**
: The onboard computer runs **AI models directly on the robot** — the intelligence is onboard, not in the cloud.

## The goal: autonomy

The destination of the whole program is **autonomous behavior**: the robot perceives with its camera and LiDAR, decides with embedded AI, and acts — with no one at the controls.
