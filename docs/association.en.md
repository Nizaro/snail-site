# The association

snAIl will be carried by a **non-profit association** (being created), so that cost is never the barrier between a young person and robotics.

## Mission

Make robotics, AI and computer-vision education accessible to **every young person aged 8 to 17**, whatever their background.

## How it works

**Hands-on workshops and clubs** in schools and community venues, supported by this site's [online lessons](lecons/index.md), which teachers use to prepare their sessions.

## Our partners

We are building the project with:

- **universities** — scientific sponsorship, workshops on campus, student mentors;
- **cities** — venues for after-school clubs, a place in local youth programs, support for equipment;
- **schools** — pilot classes from primary school to high school, time slots in or after school, teacher feedback.
